Ownership is a bundle of rights: legal title, the power to decide how an asset is used, to hire and dismiss workers, to invest surplus and to dispose of output. A share certificate, a collective-farm statute and a workers' council can all be called ownership while distributing those powers differently. The book's editorial standard draws a line between theory, intention, implementation, experience, outcome and interpretation, and warns that "stated ambition is not evidence of success". A constitution declaring the means of production publicly owned establishes a legal claim; it does not say who appointed the director.

Stalin made the same point in reverse when presenting the 1936 Soviet constitution. A programme, he said, "speaks of that which does not yet exist," whereas a constitution "must speak of that which already exists" ([Stalin, report on the draft constitution, 25 November 1936](https://www.marxists.org/reference/archive/stalin/works/1936/11/25.htm)). Article 12 then declared: "The principle applied in the U.S.S.R. is that of socialism: 'From each according to his ability, to each according to his work'" ([Constitution of the USSR, 5 December 1936](https://www.marxists.org/reference/archive/stalin/works/1936/12/05.htm)). The claim was not that abundance had arrived, but that the owning relation had changed. The Soviet textbook described socialism as based on "social ownership of the means of production in its two forms": state property and co-operative or collective farm property ([*Political Economy*, chapter 26](https://www.marxists.org/subject/economy/authors/pe/pe-ch26.htm)). Both were called socialist, and the textbook stressed what they shared: no private ownership of the large factories and banks.

Whether state ownership was socialism at all was the question of the transition debate. Lenin argued in 1921 that "state capitalism would be a step forward as compared with the present state of affairs in our Soviet Republic," naming concessions, co-operatives, commission agents and leases ([Lenin, *The Tax in Kind*, 21 April 1921](https://www.marxists.org/archive/lenin/works/1921/apr/21.htm)). State capitalism here meant state ownership and management with profits taken as state income. Pannekoek used the same words as a verdict: "the work is planned, financed and managed by the state; the directors of industry are appointed by the state and profits are considered the income of the state" ([Pannekoek, *State Capitalism and Dictatorship*, 1936](https://www.marxists.org/archive/pannekoe/1936/dictatorship.htm)). Bordiga went further, defining any system of commodity production, meaning goods made for sale, combined with wage labour as capitalist ([Bordiga, *Dialogue with Stalin*, 1952](https://www.marxists.org/archive/bordiga/works/1952/stalin.htm)). Luxemburg framed the issue as one of public life rather than title: "freedom only for the supporters of the government... is no freedom at all" ([Luxemburg, *The Russian Revolution*, ch. VIII, 1918](https://www.marxists.org/archive/luxemburg/1918/russian-revolution/ch08.htm)). The Workers' Opposition made it an industrial demand: "Professional and production unions are to be built on the basis of workers democracy, the elective principle and accountability of all organs from the bottom to the top" ([Shliapnikov and signatories, *Theses of the Workers Opposition*, 18 December 1920](https://www.marxists.org/archive/shliapnikov/1921/workers-opposition.htm)). Kollontai put the objection in a sentence: "Bureaucracy is a direct negation of mass self-activity" ([Kollontai, *The Workers' Opposition*, ch. III, 1921](https://www.marxists.org/archive/kollonta/1921/workers-opposition/ch03.htm)). These texts dispute who should exercise the rights ownership conveys; they do not show who did.

## The cooperative form

The 1935 kolkhoz statute shows a legal form that was both concession and discipline. A kolkhoz was a collective farm whose land and implements were owned jointly. The statute set land in perpetuity to the artel, the farm, allowed private plots of a quarter to a half hectare and up to a hectare in some districts, and permitted privately owned cows, pigs, sheep and hives. Wages were paid in "working days," units entered in a labour book monthly, with piecework norms setting the rate. Penalties ran from a public blackboard through fines of up to five working days to dismissal, and pilfering was called "a betrayal of the kolkhoz cause" ([Kolkhoz Statute, 17 February 1935](https://soviethistory.msu.edu/1936/second-kolkhoz-charter/second-kolkhoz-charter-texts/kolkhoz-statute/)). At the congress delegates argued over plot sizes and Stalin conceded: "The kolkhozniks want to live a decent life, and for that this 0.12 hectares is not enough" ([debate reported in *Krest'ianskaia pravda*, 27 February 1935](https://soviethistory.msu.edu/1936/second-kolkhoz-charter/second-kolkhoz-charter-texts/private-garden-plots)). Joint title did not prevent an elected chairman, brigade leader and clerk from holding daily authority, as Voronov's petition complained in 1936 ([Voronov letter, 1936](http://yupnet.org/annals/siegelbaum/English_docs/Siegelbaum_doc_57.htm)).

Rochdale offered a different bundle of rights. A consumer cooperative is a shop owned by the people who use it: they pool money, buy in bulk and receive surplus in proportion to spending rather than paying a return to outside shareholders. The 1844 rules capped interest on shares and divided surplus by purchases, while declaring an ambition: "as soon as practicable, this society shall proceed to arrange the powers of production, distribution, education, and government" ([Rochdale Society of Equitable Pioneers, rules, 1844](https://panarchy.org/variousauthors/cooperative.html)). Most of those objects were never carried out. By 1995 the International Co-operative Alliance defined a cooperative as "a jointly-owned and democratically-controlled enterprise" without calling it socialist ([Statement on the Co-operative Identity, 1995](https://panarchy.org/variousauthors/cooperative.html)). Lenin argued in 1923 that "given social ownership of the means of production... the system of civilized cooperators is the system of socialism" ([Lenin, "On Cooperation", 4 and 6 January 1923](https://www.marxists.org/archive/lenin/works/1923/jan/06.htm)). The same word named a voluntary shop, a state-directed peasant network and a predicted form of socialism. The kibbutz, a voluntary commune whose members held no private property and received no wages, shows the same gap: its 1964 guide described a general meeting and rotating committees, while Hayuta Busel's journal disputed from inside the claim that it had freed women ([*A Guide to Kibbutz*, 1964](https://archive.org/details/habo025); [Busel's journal](https://jwa.org/encyclopedia/article/busel-hayyuta)).

## Workers' self-management and its appointed director

Yugoslavia built the most elaborate legal answer. Introducing the bill on 26 June 1950, Tito said state ownership had not delivered "the factories for the workers" and that directors were still appointed from above ([Tito, 26 June 1950](https://www.marxists.org/archive/tito/1950/06/26.htm)). The Basic Law provided that state enterprises be managed by workers' collectives "in the name of the community, within the scope of the state economic plan," through elected and recallable councils and boards, with at least three quarters of board members drawn from production workers ([Basic Law of 26 June 1950](https://www.marxists.org/subject/yugoslavia/self-management/1950/06/x01.htm)). Yet the plan remained in force, and the director, appointed from above, sat on the board, hired workers and could delay decisions contrary to the plan. Tito claimed in 1957 that "more than six hundred thousand workers and employees" participated in management, a figure with no stated method, while conceding that officials "deny these rights to the workers councils" ([Tito, 25 June 1957](https://www.marxists.org/archive/tito/1957/06/25.htm)). A worker said in 1969: "That's only on paper. When the managers choose their people, workers have to obey; that's how it is here" ([worker, 4 March 1969](https://www.marxists.org/reference/archive/perlman-fredy/1969/revolutionary-movement-yugoslavia.htm)). Hoxha called the system capitalist in practice, a verdict Tito rejected; the sources cannot settle it ([Hoxha, 1978](https://www.marxists.org/reference/archive/hoxha/works/1978/yugoslavia/index.htm)).

Cuba's 2019 constitution claims several forms of ownership at once. Article 18 describes public ownership of the fundamental means of production and "the planned direction of the economy"; Article 22 recognises cooperative, private with a complementary role, mixed, and institutional and associative property, meaning property held by state institutions and associations; and Article 65 restates "from each according to their ability, to each according to their labor" ([Constitution of the Republic of Cuba, 2019](https://www.constituteproject.org/constitution/Cuba_2019)). The 2011 *Lineamientos* began an Update of the model, and the 2021 Reordering Task unified currencies and reformed wages, with Raúl Castro describing more autonomy for enterprises and local government ([PCC, *Lineamientos*, 2011](http://www.cuba.cu/gobierno/documentos/2011/ing/l160711i.html); [Raúl Castro, *Central Report*, 16 April 2021](https://en.granma.cu/cuba/2021-02-16/central-report-to-the-eighth-congress-of-the-communist-party-of-cuba)). The same report admitted "inadequate control of resources" and weak incentives. Declared property forms and observed control are different things.

## Limits of the record

No original statute or constitution was examined. The Yugoslav law is a transcription, Tito's speeches are volunteer translations, and Rochdale's rules reach this book through a website. No council minutes, ballot results, investment files or records of appointments were opened, so actual authority cannot be measured. The unions chapter names Ward's 1958 critique of the labour-managed firm, but the text is not reproduced in the source base, so no claim is made about it; Horvat's defence is likewise a pointer, not a read source ([Horvat](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL1928633W/The_political_economy_of_socialism)). The evidence supports a map of what documents say owners were entitled to do, and conflicts in which those entitlements were claimed, withheld or contested. It does not establish which form delivered control to those named in its founding text.
