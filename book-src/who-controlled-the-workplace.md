The rules of the Rochdale Society of Equitable Pioneers in 1844 promised that the society would "arrange the powers of production, distribution, education, and government, or in other words, to establish a self-supporting home colony of united interests" ([rules](https://panarchy.org/variousauthors/cooperative.html)). Most of those objects were never carried out; what survived was limited interest on shares and surplus divided according to purchases ([*The Cooperative Idea*](https://panarchy.org/variousauthors/cooperative.html)). The governing standard of this book draws a line across that gap: "Distinguish theory, intention, implementation, experience, outcome, and interpretation. Stated ambition is not evidence of success" (research/MASTER-STANDARD.md). Applying it means asking separately who was elected, who appointed directors, who set the plan and who decided the wage.

Yugoslavia built the most elaborate formal answer. The Basic Law of 26 June 1950 provided that state enterprises be managed by workers' collectives "in the name of the community, within the scope of the state economic plan", through elected and recallable workers' councils and management boards; at least three quarters of board members had to be production workers ([Basic Law](https://www.marxists.org/subject/yugoslavia/self-management/1950/06/x01.htm)). The director, however, was appointed by a higher state or economic organ, sat on the board, hired workers and could delay decisions contrary to the plan ([*The Yugoslav Experiment*](https://www.marxists.org/subject/yugoslavia/self-management/1950/06/x01.htm)). Tito told the First Congress of Workers' Councils in 1957 that "more than six hundred thousand workers and employees" participated in management, a figure with no stated method, and in the same speech conceded that "certain people in leading positions in the enterprises deny these rights to the workers councils" ([Tito](https://www.marxists.org/archive/tito/1957/06/25.htm)). In March 1969 a worker told the student paper *Student*: "That's only on paper. When the managers choose their people, workers have to obey" ([worker](https://www.marxists.org/reference/archive/perlman-fredy/1969/revolutionary-movement-yugoslavia.htm)). One unnamed person in a hostile compilation cannot stand for every factory, but the remark corroborates the sponsor's admission. The 1974 Constitution later institutionalised associated labour and delegates' assemblies ([*Global Survey*](https://www.marxists.org/subject/yugoslavia/index.htm)).

Soviet workplaces offered a different settlement. The 1935 kolkhoz statute set land in perpetuity to the farm and paid wages in "working days" entered in a labour book, with piecework norms and penalties from a public blackboard to dismissal ([statute](https://soviethistory.msu.edu/1936/second-kolkhoz-charter/second-kolkhoz-charter-texts/kolkhoz-statute/)). Delegates argued over private plots, and Stalin enlarged them against the claim that "0.12 hectares is not enough" ([debate](https://soviethistory.msu.edu/1936/second-kolkhoz-charter/second-kolkhoz-charter-texts/private-garden-plots)). The official formula, "From each according to his ability, to each according to his work", was the equality of labour that Marx had called "a bourgeois right" ([1936 constitution](https://www.marxists.org/reference/archive/stalin/works/1936/12/05.htm); [*Gotha Programme*](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1875/gotha/ch01.htm)). Stakhanovism showed the distance between a record and a norm: Stakhanov cut 102 tons in 1935 with two timberers, horse-drivers and a repair shift serving his face, and Ordzhonikidze told the Stakhanovite congress that "These 102 tons were produced by an entire brigade" ([Stakhanov](https://www.directdemocracy4u.uk/dd4en-resources/socialist-construction/The_Story_of_My_Life_A_Stakhanov_1947_E_Book_en.pdf); [Markin](https://www.marxists.org/history/etol/newspape/ni/vol03/no01/markin.htm)). Stalin called the movement "profoundly revolutionary" and "almost spontaneously... from below", while ordering party bodies to curb resistant managers ([Stalin](https://soviethistory.msu.edu/1936/year-of-the-stakhanovite/year-of-the-stakhanovite-texts/stalin-at-the-conference-of-stakhanovites/)). The exceptional result was used to raise norms. A hostile press digest collected reports of resentment, but its editor selected the items ([Markin](https://www.marxists.org/history/etol/newspape/ni/vol03/no01/markin.htm)).

Letters show the boundary of authority. P. I. Voronov asked in 1936 for work-days to be entered every ten days and complained that the chairman, brigade leader and clerk did no work ([Voronov](http://yupnet.org/annals/siegelbaum/English_docs/Siegelbaum_doc_57.htm)). V. A. Sychov reported that his brigade was "all barefoot because we have noware to buy" and that the milk plan fell to 55 to 60 per cent for want of water ([Sychov](https://soviethistory.msu.edu/1936/second-kolkhoz-charter/second-kolkhoz-charter-texts/life-of-a-herdsman/)). These are plan-fulfilling workers using the plan's language. Rationing set another limit: Ksenia Chekina, stripped of her internal passport, wrote "I have been without a job, without shelter, without a piece of bread for seven months already" ([Chekina](http://yupnet.org/annals/siegelbaum/English_docs/Siegelbaum_doc_153.htm)). China's household registration regulation of 9 January 1958 required one permanent residence and made a move to a city conditional on an employment certificate, school admission or official approval ([regulation](https://www.cecc.gov/resources/legal-provisions/regulations-on-household-registration-of-the-peoples-republic-of-china); [translation](https://www.danyoungcpa.com/regulations-household-registration-prc-1958)); no source here shows how it touched a named person. Mao's December 1958 criticism named "coercion, telling lies, and making false reports" and wondered whether dining halls would collapse if they served only cold rice ([Mao](https://www.marxists.org/reference/archive/mao/selected-works/volume-8/mswv8_23.htm)). In Cuba, Guevara placed the making of "the new man and woman" beside material foundations and subordinated material incentives to moral ones ([Guevara](https://www.marxists.org/archive/guevara/1965/03/man-socialism.htm)); posters urged people into the 1970 cane harvest, and the surviving record has no rank-and-file mill-worker oral history ([posters](https://www.marxists.org/subject/art/posters/Cuba/index.htm); [Elvy](https://acontracorriente.chass.ncsu.edu/index.php/acontracorriente/article/view/183)).

Unions claimed the same authority in another vocabulary. The Charter of Amiens of 1906 said the union, "today a resistance group will be, in the future, a group for production and redistribution" ([charter](https://www.marxists.org/history/france/cgt/charter-amiens.htm)). On the Clyde, shop stewards, workers elected by their fellows at the workplace, built a committee of 250 to 300 delegates, and Gallacher stated its aim was "to place the workers in complete control of the industry" ([Gallacher](https://www.marxists.org/archive/gallacher/1916/call-workers.htm); [committee](https://www.marxists.org/archive/gallacher/1915/clyde-committee.htm)). The IWW preamble opened "The working class and the employing class have nothing in common"; Haywood called industrial unionism "socialism with its working clothes on", while Hillquit feared a rival union would split the socialist movement ([preamble](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Songs_of_the_Workers_%289th_edition%29); [debate](https://archive.org/details/120111-hillquithaywood-nycdebate)). In Lawrence in 1912 the strike committee was not formally an IWW body, though its chairmen were members ([Neill report](https://archive.org/details/cu31924054583020); [Ebert](https://archive.org/details/trialofnewsociet00eber)). Chilean workers set up cordons in the bosses' strike of October 1972, with executives "elected in the factory assemblies" and accounting books taken over; the cordons were later squeezed between government, union federation and Communist Party ([Fernández Hermana](https://www.marxists.org/history/etol/newspape/isj/1974/no066/fernandez.htm)). Brazil's new unionism worked through factory assemblies under military rule, and the PT's founding act defended "the autonomy of unions... independent of the party itself" ([ABC strike](https://fpabramo.org.br/a-greve-de-1980/); [founding act](https://fpabramo.org.br/ata-de-fundacao-do-pt/)). In government Lula promised instead to respect contracts and keep the fiscal surplus ([letter, 2002](https://fpabramo.org.br/carta-ao-povo-brasileiro-por-luiz-inacio-lula-da-silva/)).

Cooperatives posed the sharpest test of voting power. Rochdale's principle was "one member, one vote" with limited interest on capital; the International Co-operative Alliance repeated it in 1966 and in 1995 defined a cooperative as "jointly-owned and democratically-controlled" without calling it socialist ([principles](https://panarchy.org/variousauthors/cooperative.html)). Lenin argued in 1923 that "given social ownership of the means of production... the system of civilized cooperators is the system of socialism", a programme written after power had been won ([Lenin](https://www.marxists.org/archive/lenin/works/1923/jan/06.htm)). The Lucas Aerospace stewards rejected participation as a substitute for authority: "There cannot be 'industrial democracy' until there is a real shift in power to the workers themselves" ([plan](https://archive.org/details/the-lucas-plan)); management rejected it ([Salisbury](https://www.lucasplan.org.uk/story-of-the-lucas-plan/)). Hayuta Busel disputed from inside the kibbutz the claim that it had freed women ([journal](https://jwa.org/encyclopedia/article/busel-hayyuta); [movement guide](https://archive.org/details/habo025)).

Historians do not agree what these institutions were: Yugoslav self-management as a transfer of power or a managerial class, German councils as a revolutionary alternative or a management tool, Chilean cordons as an embryo of workers' power or a short-lived response, the Lucas plan as a socialist alternative or defensive reform, the kibbutz as emancipation or collectivised domestic labour ([Azzellini](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL26446116W/An_Alternative_Labour_History); [Wainwright and Elliott](https://www.spokesmanbooks.com/acatalog/The_Lucas_Plan.html)). These are interpretive disputes over a shared record.

The absence of independent measurement is the central limit. No council minutes, ballot results or strike records were opened for Yugoslavia; the Chilean account names no rank-and-file worker; no survey measures Soviet workers' shortages or their view of the kolkhoz; no independent figure tests the Cuban harvest mobilisation or China's commune canteens ([Yugoslavia](https://www.marxists.org/reference/archive/perlman-fredy/1969/revolutionary-movement-yugoslavia.htm); [unions limits](https://archives.library.cornell.edu/repositories/4/resources/10215); [everyday life limits](https://forrester.domains.swarthmore.edu/alum-readings/2003/ginzburg.html); [Cuba limits](https://web.archive.org/web/20240405134333/https://aura.antioch.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1823&context=etds); [China limits](https://www.marxists.org/reference/archive/mao/selected-works/volume-8/mswv8_23.htm)). Almost every voice is a leader, organiser, state figure or petitioner, and most English texts are unchecked translations. What can be established is the formal distribution of authority and documented conflicts over norms, pay, housing, plots and rations. Whether workers obtained meaningful control cannot be settled by one measurement: the sources show them winning specific concessions and being overruled on who directs production and who sets the target.

For the documentary basis see [*The Workshop and the Union*](https://www.marxists.org/subject/yugoslavia/self-management/1950/06/x01.htm), [*The Cooperative Idea*](https://panarchy.org/variousauthors/cooperative.html), [*The Yugoslav Experiment*](https://www.marxists.org/archive/tito/1957/06/25.htm), [*After the Victory*](https://www.marxists.org/archive/guevara/1965/03/man-socialism.htm), [*Land to the Tiller*](https://www.cecc.gov/resources/legal-provisions/regulations-on-household-registration-of-the-peoples-republic-of-china), [*Everyday life under Stalin*](https://soviethistory.msu.edu/1936/second-kolkhoz-charter/second-kolkhoz-charter-texts/kolkhoz-statute/), [*The "state capitalist" debate and the theory of transition*](https://www.marxists.org/archive/shliapnikov/1921/workers-opposition.htm), [*One Big Union*](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Songs_of_the_Workers_%289th_edition%29) and [*Brazil*](https://fpabramo.org.br/ata-de-fundacao-do-pt/).
