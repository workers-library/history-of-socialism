On 15 March 1921, with the revolt at Kronstadt unresolved, Lenin told the Tenth Party Congress that the revolution had to give way to the peasantry. The New Economic Policy, or NEP, was that retreat: a fixed tax replacing requisitioning and permission to sell what remained. Lenin called the tax measure "primarily and mainly a political question", essentially about "the attitude of the working class to the peasantry" ([Lenin at the Tenth Party Congress, 15 March 1921](https://soviethistory.msu.edu/1921/tenth-party-congress/tenth-party-congress-texts/lenin-explains-the-tax-in-kind-and-the-new-policy)). The decree of 21 March specified that "This tax must be less than what the peasant has given up to this time through requisitions" ([Tax in Kind decree](https://soviethistory.msu.edu/1921/new-economic-policy/new-economic-policy-texts/nep-in-the-countryside/)). The change was made from weakness. J. P. Goodrich, surveying the Volga famine zone for the American Relief Administration, chose villages away from railways and found people eating cabbage leaves and melon rinds ([Goodrich, 1 November 1921](https://soviethistory.msu.edu/1921/famine-of-1921-22/famine-of-1921-22-texts/american-descriptions-of-the-volga-famine)). His figures were a relief advocate's case, but rested on houses he entered. Every later transition began in crisis and decided which groups the state would favour.

NEP made the political content visible. Lenin argued that state ownership could serve a workers' government: "State capitalism would be a step forward as compared with the present state of affairs in our Soviet Republic" ([Lenin, *The Tax in Kind*, 21 April 1921](https://www.marxists.org/archive/lenin/works/1921/apr/21.htm)). A Nepman, the word for a private trader, became a recognisable type, while Fischer remembered young workers shed first ([Fischer, 1944](https://www.thetedkarchive.com/library/markoosha-fischer-my-lives-in-russia)). By 1923 industrial prices were 276 per cent of 1913 levels against 89 per cent for farm prices, a gap called the scissors ([Siegelbaum](https://soviethistory.msu.edu/1924/scissors-crisis/)). Trotsky demanded "cheaper nails, cloth and matches" instead of repeating the *smychka* slogan, the bond between town and country ([Trotsky, 1923](https://www.marxists.org/archive/trotsky/1923/newcourse/ch06.htm)). Bukharin replied that conflict between worker and peasant was "only possible," not inevitable ([Bukharin, 1924](https://soviethistory.msu.edu/1924/scissors-crisis/scissors-crisis-texts/bukharin-on-the-worker-peasant-alliance/)). Lenin left the question open: "By adopting NEP we made a concession to the peasant as a trader" ([Lenin, "On Cooperation"](https://www.marxists.org/archive/lenin/works/1923/jan/06.htm); [Lenin, "Better Fewer, But Better"](https://soviethistory.msu.edu/1924/lenin-enrollment/lenin-enrollment-texts/better-fewer-but-better)). Lewin tests whether the last articles reorient strategy ([Lewin](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL3958717W)). The end came through everyday machinery: villagers asked how many cattle they could have before being called kulaks, peasants deemed class enemies, and after 1929 Nepmen were disenfranchised ([Ball](https://www.ucpress.edu/books/russias-last-capitalists/paper)).

## Yugoslavia: a transition without private trade

The Yugoslav transition began with an international rupture, not a market crisis. After the Soviet-led Cominform expelled the party in 1948, Tito told the Slovene Academy that "Socialism does not require of us that we renounce our love for our socialist country" ([Tito, 26 November 1948](https://www.marxists.org/archive/tito/1948/11/26.htm)). In June 1950 he introduced a law because state ownership had not delivered "the factories for the workers" ([Tito, 26 June 1950](https://www.marxists.org/archive/tito/1950/06/26.htm)). The Basic Law put enterprises under workers' collectives "in the name of the community, within the scope of the state economic plan," with elected councils but an appointed director who hired workers and could delay decisions contrary to the plan ([Basic Law, 26 June 1950](https://www.marxists.org/subject/yugoslavia/self-management/1950/06/x01.htm)). This was decentralisation inside a plan. Tito claimed in 1957 that over six hundred thousand workers participated in management while admitting officials denied councils their rights ([Tito, 25 June 1957](https://www.marxists.org/archive/tito/1957/06/25.htm)). Perlman argued that self-management masked bureaucratic power; Hoxha called it capitalist ([Perlman, 1969](https://www.marxists.org/reference/archive/perlman-fredy/1969/revolutionary-movement-yugoslavia.htm); [Hoxha, 1978](https://www.marxists.org/reference/archive/hoxha/works/1978/yugoslavia/index.htm)). No council minutes or price series decide between them. A change in formal owner can be a political realignment without a change in allocation.

## Vietnam and China: crisis and the party's timetable

Đổi Mới, meaning renewal, was adopted at Vietnam's Sixth Party Congress in December 1986 in response to crisis. Fforde records inflation above 700 percent, exports of about US$500 million against US$1,221 million in imports, and areas near famine. Output contracts let households keep a surplus; Contract 10 of 1988 gave land rights, ended central targets and allowed open sales ([Fforde, 1997](https://www.bannedthought.net/Vietnam/Academic/DoiMoi/Fforde-DoiMoi-TenYearsAfter1986Congress-1997-OCR-sm.pdf)). Transition reached the household. Article 51 of the 2013 constitution calls the result a "socialist oriented market economy" with the state sector leading ([Vietnam's 2013 constitution](https://www.constituteproject.org/constitution/Socialist_Republic_of_Vietnam_2013)). Werner argued subcontracting weakened collectives and shifted women's work home; Fforde found the evidence thin ([Werner, 1984](https://doi.org/10.1080/14672715.1984.10409805); [Fforde's response, 1985](https://vuir.vu.edu.au/31724/)). The 2026 Fourteenth Congress set targets of ten percent growth and high income status by 2045, promises rather than results ([14th Congress resolution](https://en.baochinhphu.vn/full-resolution-of-14th-national-party-congress-111260214171856653.htm)).

China's reform settlement followed another sequence. The 1982 constitution followed the Third Plenary Session of December 1978 and the 1981 Resolution repudiating the Cultural Revolution while defending Mao's place ([1981 Resolution](https://www.marxists.org/subject/china/documents/cpc/history/01.htm)). Amendments in 1988 allowed private enterprise; in 1993 they described the "primary stage of socialism," a long early period needing markets, and renamed the goal a "socialist market economy" ([official constitutional text](https://english.www.gov.cn/archive/lawsregulations/201911/20/content_WS5ed8856ec6d0b3f0e9499913.html)). The 2024 Third Plenum set 2035 for finishing a high-standard socialist market economy ([communiqué, 18 July 2024](https://english.www.gov.cn/news/202407/18/content_WS669906cdc6d0868f4e8e93db.html)). These are official goals, untested by household surveys here.

## Cuba and North Korea: repair after shock

Cuba's turn followed the Special Period, the emergency after Soviet collapse cut off subsidies. Perez-Lopez records contraction by a third or more by 1993, and Mesa-Lago found Havana rations covered half of recommended calories ([Perez-Lopez](https://ascecubadatabase.org/asce_proceedings/the-cuban-economic-crisis-of-the-1990s-and-the-external-sector); [Mesa-Lago](https://repositorio.cepal.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/3cd9b743-ba48-4d96-b9a9-b92185af21e2/content)). The 2011 *Lineamientos* began an Update, and the 2021 Reordering Task unified currencies and wages. Raúl Castro described "an appropriate combination of centralized planning and decentralization," ruled out shock therapies, and admitted "excessive bureaucracy, inadequate control of resources" and a model that "does not provide sufficient incentives for work or innovation" ([Raúl Castro, *Central Report*, 2021](https://en.granma.cu/cuba/2021-04-22/central-report-to-the-eighth-congress-of-the-communist-party-of-cuba)). Farber read them as a turn toward Sino-Vietnamese state capitalism, a claim beyond the documents ([review of Farber](https://www.foreignaffairs.com/reviews/capsule-review/2012-01-01/cuba-revolution-1959-critical-assessment)). North Korea is a counter-case: Kim Il Sung said in 1982 that the economy was "unaffected by world economic fluctuations," yet the mid-1990s brought one of the century's worst famines ([Kim Il Sung, 1982](https://www.marxists.org/archive/kim-il-sung/2020/20200729120144.pdf)). Haggard and Noland see later reforms as reactive control of an economy partly marketised from below; Smith stresses changed interests ([Haggard and Noland, 2007](https://www.piie.com/bookstore/famine-north-korea-markets-aid-and-reform); [Smith, 2015](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/north-korea/C2E4A593E3BB7FF436F32F3566168AFF)). Both accept informal markets grew.

## 1989 to 1991: transition as political breakup

The end of the Soviet Union was a transition through political forms. The March 1991 referendum asked whether to preserve "the Union of Soviet Socialist Republics as a renewed federation of equal sovereign republics"; 76.43 per cent of participants in nine republics voted yes ([referendum record](https://soviethistory.msu.edu/1991/march-referendum)). The draft treaty of 15 August made each republic "a sovereign state" delegating listed powers; it was never signed ([draft treaty](https://soviethistory.msu.edu/1991/nine-plus-one-agreement/nine-plus-one-agreement-texts/new-union-treaty/)). Gorbachev's resignation said "Events have taken a different path" and blamed a policy of dismemberment ([Gorbachev, 25 December 1991](https://soviethistory.msu.edu/1991/end-of-the-soviet-union/end-of-the-soviet-union-texts/gorbachev-resigns-as-president)). The Belovezh accord of 8 December declared the USSR "ceases to exist" ([Belovezh Accords](https://eccis.org/en/page/176)). Historians agree on the sequence, not the weight: Plokhy stresses national disintegration, Zubok economic factors and Russian choices, Savranskaya and Blanton that American policy backed the centre rather than engineering the breakup ([National Security Archive, 2021](https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/briefing-book/russia-programs/2021-12-21/end-soviet-union-1991)). In East Germany the New Forum appealed in September 1989 to the regime's own constitution: "Communication between the state and society has obviously broken down in our country" ([New Forum, 9 September 1989](https://germanhistorydocs.org/en/a-new-germany-1990-2023/founding-appeal-of-the-new-forum-september-9-1989)). Steiner explains GDR failure through planning, Gieseke through repression, social historians through accommodation ([Steiner](https://www.berghahnbooks.com/title/SteinerPlans); [Gieseke](https://www.berghahnbooks.com/title/GiesekeHistory)). These are disagreements about evidence and emphasis, not equal claims.

In theory, Lenin treated state capitalism as a step forward; Pannekoek called Soviet industry state capitalism; Bordiga held that commodity production and wage labour made it capitalist regardless of title ([Lenin](https://www.marxists.org/archive/lenin/works/1921/apr/21.htm); [Pannekoek, 1936](https://www.marxists.org/archive/pannekoe/1936/dictatorship.htm); [Bordiga, 1952](https://www.marxists.org/archive/bordiga/works/1952/stalin.htm)). Luxemburg insisted that a transition without public freedom was not the socialism she argued for ([Luxemburg, 1918](https://www.marxists.org/archive/luxemburg/1918/russian-revolution/ch06.htm)). The dispute is political: it determines who decides, a party, a council, a household or a market. The sources record arguments and crises, not evidence that any path was inevitable or any outcome matched its authors' intent.

## Limits of the record

No archive was used and most documents are translations; originals were not consulted. The record is strongest on official decisions and leaders' speeches, weakest on household experience. Fforde's figures come from an economist, Cuban contraction figures from conference papers, scissors prices from a historian and North Korean deaths from incomplete evidence. No enterprise records or rank-and-file testimony test the official claims. The classic calculation literature is absent, so planning's theoretical case cannot be settled here. The evidence supports documented crossings, each driven by crisis and decided through political struggle over who bore the cost. Causes and outcomes remain contested.
