# Verification-Tagged Bibliography: Parts IV–XVII
***History of Socialism* Master Reference Guide**

Every entry is categorized by its evidentiary tier to maintain strict verification standards:
* **`[Empirical Dataset]`**: Standardized historical national accounts, distributional series, or macroeconomic panels with published methodology.
* **`[Primary Official]`**: State documents, statutes, central planning directives, legal inquiries, or official economic statistics.
* **`[Primary Theoretical]`**: Foundational treatises, theoretical models, and polemics in verified full-text editions.
* **`[Scholarly Secondary]`**: Peer-reviewed monographs, journal articles, and analytical histories.

## Part IV: The Origins and Development of Socialist Thought

* **`[babeuf_1796_manifesto]`** `[Primary Theoretical]` — Babeuf, Gracchus (1796). *Manifesto of the Equals* (*Manifeste des Égaux*). Paris: Conspiracy of the Equals (`marxists.org`). URL: `https://www.marxists.org/history/france/revolution/babeuf/1796/manifesto-equals.htm`
  * *Scope & Caveats:* Early revolutionary communist text demanding absolute material equality, land commonality, and the abolition of private property.
* **`[owen_1813_newview]`** `[Primary Theoretical]` — Owen, Robert (1813). *A New View of Society; or, Essays on the Principle of the Formation of the Human Character*. London: Cadell and Davies (Online Library of Liberty). URL: `https://oll.libertyfund.org/titles/owen-a-new-view-of-society`
  * *Scope & Caveats:* Foundational utopian socialist treatise on industrial character formation, cooperative communities, and education reform.
* **`[fourier_1808_theory]`** `[Primary Theoretical]` — Fourier, Charles (1808 / 1996). *The Theory of the Four Movements*, ed. Gareth Stedman Jones and Ian Patterson. Cambridge: Cambridge University Press. ISBN: `978-0-521-35693-0`.
  * *Scope & Caveats:* Primary treatise outlining phalansterian association, non-coercive labor, and passion-based social organization.

## Part V: The Intellectual Traditions of Socialism

* **`[marx_engels_1848_manifesto]`** `[Primary Theoretical]` — Marx, Karl, and Friedrich Engels (1848). *Manifesto of the Communist Party*. London: Workers' Educational Association (`marxists.org`). URL: `https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1848/communist-manifesto/`
  * *Scope & Caveats:* Classical statement of historical materialism, class struggle, and socialist political strategy.
* **`[bakunin_1873_statism]`** `[Primary Theoretical]` — Bakunin, Mikhail (1873 / 1990). *Statism and Anarchy*, ed. Marshall Shatz. Cambridge: Cambridge University Press. ISBN: `978-0-521-36973-2`.
  * *Scope & Caveats:* Foundational anarchist critique of authoritarian socialism, state dictatorship, and Marxist party organization.
* **`[kautsky_1892_erfurt]`** `[Primary Theoretical]` — Kautsky, Karl (1892). *The Class Struggle (Erfurt Program)*, trans. William E. Bohn (`marxists.org`). URL: `https://www.marxists.org/archive/kautsky/1892/erfurt/`
  * *Scope & Caveats:* Orthodox Marxist theoretical synthesis of social-democratic party strategy and orthodox historical development.
* **`[bernstein_1899_evolutionary]`** `[Primary Theoretical]` — Bernstein, Eduard (1899 / 1993). *The Preconditions of Socialism* (*Die Voraussetzungen des Sozialismus*), ed. Henry Tudor. Cambridge: Cambridge University Press. ISBN: `978-0-521-39808-4`.
  * *Scope & Caveats:* Foundational revisionist text arguing for gradual reform, parliamentary democracy, and the abandonment of breakdown theory.

## Part VI: Workers' Movements, Trade Unions and Reform

  * *Scope & Caveats:* Foundational Fabian institutional history of British trade union governance, collective bargaining, and labor legislation.
* **`[gramsci_1919_factorycouncils]`** `[Primary Theoretical]` — Gramsci, Antonio (1919–1920). *Workers' Councils and the Factory Control*, articles in *L'Ordine Nuovo* (`marxists.org`). URL: `https://www.marxists.org/archive/gramsci/1919/06/factory-councils.htm`
  * *Scope & Caveats:* Primary documents from the Turin factory occupations, advocating workers' council control over industrial production.

## Part IX: Socialist Political Economy (Calculation, Incentives, Shortage & Self-Management)

* **`[mises_1920_calculation]`** `[Primary Theoretical]` — Mises, Ludwig von (1920). "Die Wirtschaftsrechnung im sozialistischen Gemeinwesen." *Archiv für Sozialwissenschaft und Sozialpolitik*, Vol. 47, pp. 86–121; trans. S. Adler as *Economic Calculation in the Socialist Commonwealth* (1935 / Mises Institute). URL: `https://mises.org/library/economic-calculation-socialist-commonwealth`
  * *Scope & Caveats:* Establishes the socialist calculation argument: without private property in capital goods, market prices cannot form, rendering rational capital allocation impossible.
* **`[hayek_1945_knowledge]`** `[Primary Theoretical]` — Hayek, Friedrich A. (1945). "The Use of Knowledge in Society." *American Economic Review*, Vol. 35, No. 4, pp. 519–530. URL: `https://www.econlib.org/library/Essays/hykKnl.html`
  * *Scope & Caveats:* Epistemological argument that dispersed, local, time-and-place knowledge cannot be aggregated by central planners; prices act as a dynamic communication system.
* **`[lange_1936_theory1]`** `[Primary Theoretical]` — Lange, Oskar (1936–1937). "On the Economic Theory of Socialism: Parts I & II." *Review of Economic Studies*, Vol. 4, No. 1, pp. 53–71; Vol. 4, No. 2, pp. 123–142.
  * *Scope & Caveats:* Formulates market-socialist trial-and-error price adjustment via a Central Planning Board setting accounting prices to match marginal cost.
* **`[lerner_1944_economics]`** `[Primary Theoretical]` — Lerner, Abba P. (1944). *The Economics of Control: Principles of Welfare Economics*. New York: Macmillan.
  * *Scope & Caveats:* Formal mathematical treatment of welfare rules for public enterprises in a controlled market economy.
* **`[ward_1958_illyria]`** `[Primary Theoretical]` — Ward, Benjamin (1958). "The Firm in Illyria: Market Syndicalism." *American Economic Review*, Vol. 48, No. 4, pp. 566–589.
  * *Scope & Caveats:* Microeconomic model of worker-managed firms showing a backward-bending supply curve under labor-per-worker maximization.
* **`[robinson_1962_philosophy]`** `[Primary Theoretical]` — Robinson, Joan (1962). *Economic Philosophy*. London: C.A. Watts & Co.
  * *Scope & Caveats:* Cambridge post-Keynesian critique of both neoclassical utility theory and orthodox Marxist value theory.
* **`[kornai_1980_shortage]`** `[Primary Theoretical]` — Kornai, János (1980). *Economics of Shortage* (2 vols.). Amsterdam: North-Holland Publishing Company. ISBN: `978-0-444-85426-1`.
  * *Scope & Caveats:* Foundational institutional analysis of the Soft Budget Constraint, paternalistic state firms, and chronic shortage dynamics in centrally planned economies.
* **`[horvat_1982_politicaleconomy]`** `[Primary Theoretical]` — Horvat, Branko (1982). *The Political Economy of Socialism: A Theory of Democratic Socialism*. Armonk, NY: M.E. Sharpe. ISBN: `978-0-87332-256-0`.
  * *Scope & Caveats:* Theoretical synthesis of Yugoslav self-management political economy, social ownership, and labor-managed planning.
* **`[nove_1983_feasible]`** `[Scholarly Secondary]` — Nove, Alec (1983). *The Economics of Feasible Socialism*. London: George Allen & Unwin. ISBN: `978-0-04-335049-2`.
  * *Scope & Caveats:* Empirical assessment of Soviet planning limits, establishing why complex modern industrial economies require combined market and state allocation.
* **`[ostrom_1990_commons]`** `[Scholarly Secondary]` — Ostrom, Elinor (1990). *Governing the Commons: The Evolution of Institutions for Collective Action*. Cambridge: Cambridge University Press. ISBN: `978-0-521-40599-7`.
  * *Scope & Caveats:* Nobel Prize-winning empirical analysis demonstrating that community self-governance can successfully manage common-pool resources without state nationalization or private enclosure.
* **`[stiglitz_1994_whither]`** `[Scholarly Secondary]` — Stiglitz, Joseph E. (1994). *Whither Socialism?*. Cambridge, MA: MIT Press. ISBN: `978-0-262-19340-5`.
  * *Scope & Caveats:* Demonstrates that both central planning and standard neoclassical Lange-market models fail due to asymmetric information, principal-agent problems, and incomplete risk markets.

## Part X: National Liberation and Anti-Colonial Movements

* **`[cabral_1966_tricontinental]`** `[Primary Theoretical]` — Cabral, Amílcar (1966). *The Weapon of Theory*, address to the First Tricontinental Conference in Havana (`marxists.org`). URL: `https://www.marxists.org/subject/africa/cabral/1966/weapon-theory.htm`
  * *Scope & Caveats:* Analysis of national liberation as an expression of a people's re-entry into their own history through structural anti-imperialism.
* **`[sankara_1983_political]`** `[Primary Theoretical]` — Sankara, Thomas (1983). *Political Orientation Speech* (*Discours d'orientation politique*). Ouagadougou: National Council of the Revolution (`marxists.org`). URL: `https://www.marxists.org/archive/sankara/1983/october/political-orientation.htm`
  * *Scope & Caveats:* Primary program for agrarian self-sufficiency, anti-debt resistance, environmental reforestation, and gender emancipation in Burkina Faso.

## Part XI: Everyday Life Under Different Economic Systems

* **`[fitzpatrick_1999_everyday]`** `[Scholarly Secondary]` — Fitzpatrick, Sheila (1999). *Everyday Stalinism: Ordinary Life in Extraordinary Times: Soviet Russia in the 1930s*. Oxford: Oxford University Press. ISBN: `978-0-19-505001-1`.
  * *Scope & Caveats:* Social history of urban everyday life under Soviet central planning, detailing informal exchange (*blat*), rationing, housing shortages, and state surveillance.
* **`[ledeneva_1998_russia]`** `[Scholarly Secondary]` — Ledeneva, Alena V. (1998). *Russia's Economy of Favours: Blat, Networking and Informal Exchange*. Cambridge: Cambridge University Press. ISBN: `978-0-521-62743-6`.
  * *Scope & Caveats:* Empirical study of informal network economies, personal ties, and survival strategies under state socialism.

## Part XII: Repression, Resistance and Political Freedom

* **`[conquest_1968_greatterror]`** `[Scholarly Secondary]` — Conquest, Robert (1968 / 2008). *The Great Terror: A Reassessment*. Oxford: Oxford University Press. ISBN: `978-0-19-531699-5`.
  * *Scope & Caveats:* Historiographical study of Soviet political purges, show trials, and labor camps; compare post-1991 Soviet archival releases (e.g. Getty, Wheatcroft) for precise numerical estimates.
* **`[getty_1993_victimsscales]`** `[Scholarly Secondary]` — Getty, J. Arch, Gábor T. Rittersporn, and Viktor N. Zemskov (1993). "Victims of the Soviet Penal System in the Pre-War Years: A First Approach on the Basis of Archival Evidence." *American Historical Review*, Vol. 98, No. 4, pp. 1017–1049.
  * *Scope & Caveats:* First systematic analysis of declassified OGPU/NKVD/GULAG archival records detailing prisoner numbers, executions, and camp mortality (1930–1953).

## Part XIII: Social Democracy, Cooperatives and Alternative Socialisms

* **`[rehn_meidner_1951_model]`** `[Primary Official]` — Rehn, Gösta, and Rudolf Meidner (1951). *Trade Unions and Full Employment* (*Fackföreningsrörelsen och den fulla sysselsättningen*). Stockholm: Landsorganisationen i Sverige (LO).
  * *Scope & Caveats:* Swedish LO model for solidaristic wage bargaining, active labor market policies, and inflation control under social democracy.
* **`[meidner_1978_employee]`** `[Primary Theoretical]` — Meidner, Rudolf (1978). *Employee Investment Funds: An Approach to Collective Capital Formation*. London: George Allen & Unwin. ISBN: `978-0-04-331072-4`.
  * *Scope & Caveats:* Proposal for gradual wage-earner funds designed to transfer corporate equity ownership to worker collective funds in Sweden.
