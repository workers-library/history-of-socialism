# Independent verification of author-collection corrections

**Date:** 26 November 2026
**Scope:** Independent re-check of corrections listed in `research/AUTHOR-COLLECTION-PROGRESS.md`, "Corrections made during research". This is an audit of the corrections only, not new research on whole authors.
**Method:** Each entry below was checked against a source opened directly in this pass: publisher catalogues and author pages, Crossref metadata, library and archive catalogue APIs, and journal/host pages. The dossiers and progress tracker were read first to identify the claims; every URL in the table was then opened here and the quoted wording comes from those opened pages, not from the dossiers. Search engines were used only as discovery aids and, where they were challenged or blocked, were not used as evidence. No Wikipedia, Goodreads, Scribd or Google Books page was opened as evidence.

**Result: 22 corrections checked. No correction was found to be wrong. No profile or dossier needs changing on the basis of this audit.**

## Notes

1. **Verdict wording for rejected titles.** A verdict of "confirmed" on entries 2, 10, 12–15, 17–19 and 21–22 means the *correction* is correct: the title or attribution should stay out of the profile. It does not claim that a work with that title cannot exist anywhere; the evidence is absence from the author's own list, the publisher's catalogue, or a registration database that was actually opened. The underlying titles remain unverified, exactly as the dossiers record them.

2. **Positive attributions are stronger than negative ones.** Entries 1, 3–9, 11, 16 and 20 rest on an affirmative statement in a publisher, journal, author or institutional record: a named author, a named date or an exact title. These are the entries that would have forced a profile change if contradicted, and none was.

3. **No discrepancy found.** Nothing opened in this audit contradicts a correction in the progress tracker. In particular:
   - *Monopoly Capital* is Baran and Sweezy; Magdoff is a co-author of later books, not of that one.
   - Foster's co-author is McChesney, the article is September 1999 and omits "the", and the climate book is *Capitalism in the Anthropocene*.
   - Fisher was born in 1968 and the essay is "Exiting the Vampire Castle".
   - The "imperial mode of living" is Brand and Wissen's book, not a Hickel paper.
   - Amin's memoir is *A Life Looking Forward* (Zed, 2006).
   - The Burma book is Martin Smith's, not Pilger's.

4. **Sources blocked or timed out.** Two attempts did not yield readable text and are not counted as evidence: `https://monthlyreviewarchives.org/mr/search/search?query=%22The+Current+Economic+Crisis%22` (request timed out, twice across different endpoints) and the ZNetwork article/search pages used for entry 21 (HTTP 403). The Monthly Review main-site search and Crossref were substituted for the first and second respectively.

5. **Discovery only.** DuckDuckGo HTML returned a bot challenge and Mojeek returned a CAPTCHA; neither produced search results used here. The Verso product URL for entry 11 was recovered from the publisher's own search-page HTML, then opened directly. No search-result snippet is quoted as evidence in this file.

6. **Corrections not independently re-checked in this pass.** The tracker also lists Hudson's *Geopolitics*, Blum's *Hope Crushed*, Rodney's "output of exploitation" wording and Luxemburg's 1917 composition date. Their dossier entries were read while preparing this audit, but no new primary source was opened specifically for them, so they are not included in the 22. Federici's commons title (entry 22) and the Angela Davis title (entry 15) were re-checked and are included.

7. **No files were changed.** This audit wrote only this file. No published profile, dossier, index or generated page was edited.
