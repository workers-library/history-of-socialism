# Verified bibliography: Parts I–III

**Date:** 2026-11-24
**Provenance:** compiled from a verification dossier supplied for this project. "Verified" means checked by that dossier or by a direct fetch during integration, as recorded in `research/capitalism-source-companion.md`. PDF endpoints were checked as URLs only; their contents were not re-read here. The project methodology describes the limits of its own audit.

Every entry carries one of four evidentiary tags:

* **`[Empirical Dataset]`**: standardised historical national accounts, trade or balance-of-payments series, or distributional accounts with documented methodology.
* **`[Primary Official]`**: parliamentary debates, statutory acts, government or commission inquiries, treaties, or declassified state records.
* **`[Primary Theoretical]`**: original treatises, essays and economic critiques in verified full-text scholarly or archival editions.
* **`[Scholarly Secondary]`**: peer-reviewed articles and foundational historiographical monographs.

Tertiary and catalog stubs, Wikipedia entries, Goodreads listings, Scribd/bookshop pages and opinion pieces are excluded.

## Cross-Cutting Quantitative & Macroeconomic Datasets (Parts I–III)

* **`[bolt_vanzanden_2024_mpd]`** `[Empirical Dataset]` — Bolt, Jutta, and Jan Luiten van Zanden (2024). "Maddison style estimates of the evolution of the world economy: A new 2023 update." *Journal of Economic Surveys*, Vol. 39, No. 2 (2025), pp. 631–671. DOI: `10.1111/joes.12618` | Dataset DOI: `10.34894/inzbf2` | URL: [https://www.rug.nl/ggdc/historicaldevelopment/maddison/releases/maddison-project-database-2023?lang=en](https://www.rug.nl/ggdc/historicaldevelopment/maddison/releases/maddison-project-database-2023?lang=en)
* *Scope & Caveats:* Covers real GDP per capita and population across 169 countries (1 CE–2022). Earlier 2018/2020 GGDC releases should be cited explicitly if referencing 1990 or 2011 benchmark prices.

* **`[feenstra_etal_2025_pwt11]`** `[Empirical Dataset]` — Feenstra, Robert C., Robert Inklaar, and Marcel P. Timmer (2025). *Penn World Table Version 11.0*. Groningen Growth and Development Centre (GGDC), published October 7, 2025 (CC BY 4.0). URL: [https://www.rug.nl/ggdc/productivity/pwt/?lang=en](https://www.rug.nl/ggdc/productivity/pwt/?lang=en)
* *Scope & Caveats:* Covers 185 countries (1950–2023), separating expenditure-side living standards (`RGDPe`) from output-side productive capacity (`RGDPo`), capital stocks and labour shares. PWT 11.0 switches China to official national statistical series rather than the Maddison/Wu adjusted series used in earlier versions.

* **`[irarrazaval_2026_wil22]`** `[Empirical Dataset]` — Irarrázaval, Andrés (2026). *The Hidden Great Levelling: Subsistence, Growth, and Surplus Distribution*. World Inequality Lab Working Paper No. 2026/22 (November 2026). URL: [https://wid.world/document/the-hidden-great-levelling-subsistence-growth-and-surplus-distribution-world-inequality-lab-working-paper-2026-22/](https://wid.world/document/the-hidden-great-levelling-subsistence-growth-and-surplus-distribution-world-inequality-lab-working-paper-2026-22/)
* *Scope & Caveats:* Models top 1% surplus capture relative to basic-needs subsistence across 48 countries (85% of world population, 1820–present). Finds that pre-distribution (rising worker bargaining power as industrialisation concentrated workers) drove roughly nine-tenths of the decline in elite surplus capture, whereas fiscal taxes and transfers accounted for roughly one-tenth. Preliminary working paper extending post-tax series back from 1980 to 1820.

* **`[nievas_piketty_2025_wil11]`** `[Empirical Dataset]` — Nievas, Gaston, and Thomas Piketty (2025). *Unequal Exchange and North-South Relations: Evidence from Global Trade Flows and the World Balance of Payments, 1800–2025*. World Inequality Lab Working Paper No. 2025/11 (May 27, 2025) / World Historical Balance of Payments Database (`wbop.world`). URL: [https://wid.world/document/unequal-exchange-and-north-south-relations-evidence-from-global-trade-flows-and-the-world-balance-of-payments-1800-2025-world-inequality-lab-working-paper-2025-11/](https://wid.world/document/unequal-exchange-and-north-south-relations-evidence-from-global-trade-flows-and-the-world-balance-of-payments-1800-2025-world-inequality-lab-working-paper-2025-11/)
* *Scope & Caveats:* Covers 57 core territories (1800–2025) and 216 jurisdictions (1970–2025). Historical balance-of-payments records cover 70–80% of world GDP over 1800–2025 (95–98%+ post-1990), with less systematic pre-1970 coverage in non-Western territories.

* **`[andreescu_sodano_2026_dina]`** `[Empirical Dataset]` — Andreescu, Marie Renée, and Alice Sodano (2026). *2026 Regional DINA Update for Europe*. World Inequality Lab Technical Note No. 2026/10 (November 2026). URL: [https://wid.world/document/2026-dina-update-for-europe/](https://wid.world/document/2026-dina-update-for-europe/)
* *Scope & Caveats:* Distributional National Accounts across 38 European countries through 2025 (integrating 2024 EU-SILC/LIS waves and long-run Eastern European reconstructions). Non-EU Eastern European survey quality varies across periods.

* **`[owid_colonies]`** `[Empirical Dataset]` — Our World in Data / Clio Infra (n.d.). *European Overseas Colonies by Colonizer*. URL: [https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/european-overseas-colonies-by-colonizer](https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/european-overseas-colonies-by-colonizer)
* *Scope & Caveats:* Comparative longitudinal series of formal overseas colonial possessions by imperial power.

## Part I: The Historical Origins of Capitalism

* **`[smith_1776_wn]`** `[Primary Theoretical]` — Smith, Adam (1776 / 1904). *An Inquiry into the Nature and Causes of the Wealth of Nations*, ed. Edwin Cannan, 2 vols. London: Methuen & Co. (Online Library of Liberty). URL: [https://oll.libertyfund.org/titles/smith-an-inquiry-into-the-nature-and-causes-of-the-wealth-of-nations-cannan-ed-in-2-vols](https://oll.libertyfund.org/titles/smith-an-inquiry-into-the-nature-and-causes-of-the-wealth-of-nations-cannan-ed-in-2-vols)
* *Scope & Caveats:* Core primary source on division of labour, merchant monopolism, the Law of Settlements restricting labour mobility (`WN IV.ii.1`, `42–43`), and colonial mercantile policy (`WN IV.vii`).

* **`[proudhon_1840_property]`** `[Primary Theoretical]` — Proudhon, Pierre-Joseph (1840 / 1876). *What Is Property? An Inquiry into the Principle of Right and of Government*, trans. Benjamin R. Tucker (Project Gutenberg EBook #360). URL: [https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/360](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/360)
* *Scope & Caveats:* Foundational mutualist critique of private property rights, rent extraction and the appropriation of collective labour power (especially Chapter IV).

* **`[marx_1859_preface]`** `[Primary Theoretical]` — Marx, Karl (1859). *Preface to A Contribution to the Critique of Political Economy*, in *Karl Marx: Selected Works* (`marxists.org`). URL: [https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/download/pdf/selected-works-karl-marx.pdf](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/download/pdf/selected-works-karl-marx.pdf)
* *Scope & Caveats:* Classical statement of historical materialism, property relations and modes of production. Cross-check digital transcriptions against *Marx-Engels Collected Works* for direct quotations.

* **`[marx_1867_capital1]`** `[Primary Theoretical]` — Marx, Karl (1867). *Capital: A Critique of Political Economy*, Volume I (Part VIII: "So-Called Primitive Accumulation" and Ch. 10: "The Working Day"), in *Karl Marx: Selected Works* (`marxists.org`). URL: [https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/download/pdf/selected-works-karl-marx.pdf](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/download/pdf/selected-works-karl-marx.pdf)
* *Scope & Caveats:* Anchors debates on agrarian dispossession, enclosures, wage dependency, surplus value and working-time regulation.

* **`[polanyi_1944_gt]`** `[Primary Theoretical]` — Polanyi, Karl (1944 / 2001). *The Great Transformation: The Political and Economic Origins of Our Time*, foreword by Joseph E. Stiglitz, introduction by Fred Block. Boston: Beacon Press (`inctpped.org` PDF). URL: [https://www.inctpped.org/spiderweb/pdf_4/Great_Transformation.pdf](https://www.inctpped.org/spiderweb/pdf_4/Great_Transformation.pdf)
* *Scope & Caveats:* Contrasts pre-market reciprocity, redistribution and householding with modern "Market Society" and the commodification of land, labour and money.

* **`[harvey_2003_imperialism]`** `[Scholarly Secondary]` — Harvey, David (2003). *The New Imperialism* (Chapter 4: "Accumulation by Dispossession"). Oxford: Oxford University Press. ISBN: `978-0-19-926431-5`.
* *Scope & Caveats:* Extends Marx's primitive accumulation to ongoing privatisation, asset stripping and spatial or urban enclosures.

* **`[federici_2004_caliban]`** `[Scholarly Secondary]` — Federici, Silvia (2004). *Caliban and the Witch: Women, the Body and Primitive Accumulation*. Brooklyn, NY: Autonomedia (`libcom.org` full PDF). URL: [https://files.libcom.org/files/Caliban%20and%20the%20Witch.pdf](https://files.libcom.org/files/Caliban%20and%20the%20Witch.pdf)
* *Scope & Caveats:* Feminist revision of primitive accumulation linking enclosures and colonial expropriation to the sexual division of labour, unwaged reproductive work and 16th–17th-century European witch trials.
