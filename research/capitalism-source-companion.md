## Editorial rule

The dossier's own warning is adopted here: publisher abstracts, Wikipedia summaries, op-eds and bookseller listings confirm metadata only. A source enters a chapter only when the URL has been opened and the relevant passage read, or when a chapter already cited it. Everything else remains on this acquisition list.
