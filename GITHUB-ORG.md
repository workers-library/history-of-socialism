## Organization README

```markdown
# Workers Library

Workers Library publishes free educational books on the history of socialism,
communism, Marxism, labour movements and political ideas.

Our books are written for readers who have never formally studied political
history. We use primary sources, contemporary reporting and historical
scholarship. We show how political ideas developed, how movements understood
them, how states implemented them, and how ordinary people experienced the
results.

We hold to a few simple rules:

- Primary sources are central, and every source is evaluated rather than trusted.
- Where evidence is contested, we say what is known, what is uncertain and what
  historians disagree about.
- We include the experiences of workers, peasants, women, prisoners and critics,
  not only leaders and official documents.
- We write in plain English and define technical terms when they first appear.
- We never invent quotations, dates, page numbers or sources.

All books are free to read online. Corrections and contributions are welcome.
```

## Book description for the repository README

This book explains socialism and communism through documentary evidence. It covers the development of socialist ideas from the early nineteenth century to the present, the lives and writings of major thinkers and organizers, the parties and movements that carried those ideas, the states that attempted to build socialism, and the experiences of ordinary people who lived through those events.

Each chapter is written as documentary history for a general reader. Claims are linked to the sources that support them, and a full bibliography is provided. Where historians disagree, the disagreement is explained with its evidence and method. Where the evidence is thin, the book says so.

The book is free to read online and is built as a static site so that it can be copied, mirrored and preserved.
